Privacy Policy

For Boutique House and Seasonal.Studio

1. What this policy is

This page explains how we use, store, and protect your personal information — your name, your email, your address, or anything else you choose to share with us.

It also explains your rights and how to contact us if you ever want to update, change, or remove your information. We’ve kept it short, clear, and free from unnecessary legal language.

2. The essentials

We collect information for two reasons:

  • To run our services — for example, to process your order or deliver your purchase.
  • To improve your experience — understanding what works on our website and what doesn’t.

We never sell your data. We never share it without good reason. We always use it legally and responsibly.

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email.

3. What we collect

  • Name and contact details
  • Delivery and billing addresses
  • Email address (for newsletters, updates, or account access)
  • Payment details (processed securely by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers)
  • Order history
  • How you use our websites (pages visited, device type, browser type)
  • Your preferences (for example, whether you want to receive our emails)

We also use cookies to help the site function, understand usage, and improve performance.

4. How we collect it

  • Directly from you — when you place an order, create an account, or subscribe.
  • Automatically — through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Through trusted partners — such as payment and email providers.

5. How we use your information

  • To register and manage your account
  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To contact you about your purchase
  • To send updates or newsletters (only if you opt in)
  • To improve our websites and services
  • To keep things secure and running smoothly

We only use your data for the purpose it was collected for, or for a closely related purpose.

6. Who we share it with

We only share your information with trusted partners who help us run our services, such as:

  • Payment processors
  • Email and notification services
  • Delivery and logistics partners
  • Website hosting and security providers

They only use your data for the tasks we ask them to perform. We never sell your information.

7. How we keep your data safe

We use secure systems, encrypted connections, and trusted providers to protect your information. If anything ever poses a risk to you, we will let you know.

8. How long we keep your information

  • Order information: up to 6 years (to meet legal and accounting requirements)
  • Account information: until you ask us to delete it
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics data: kept for limited, anonymised periods

You can ask us to delete your information at any time, where we are legally able to do so.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the information we hold about you
  • Correct it if it is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete it
  • Limit or stop how we use it
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails

We aim to respond within 30 days.

10. Contact us

If you have questions or would like to exercise your rights, you can contact us at:

privacy@boutiquehouse.co.uk

privacy@seasonal.studio

11. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and highlight any important changes.
