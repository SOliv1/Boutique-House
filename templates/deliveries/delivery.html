Boutique House service

Delivery

At Boutique House, we understand that life moves quickly. Your order should arrive in a way that feels simple, calm, and convenient. We offer two delivery options depending on the size of your items.

Courier Delivery — Small & Lightweight Items

Smaller pieces such as accessories, lighting, textiles, and select seating are delivered through our trusted courier partners across most UK mainland postcodes. Standard delivery typically arrives within 5 working days.

Standard Delivery

  • Free on orders over £150
  • £6.95 for orders under £150
  • Available to most UK postcodes, including Northern Ireland

Next-Day Delivery

  • £9.95 for eligible small-parcel items
  • Order before 2pm on a working day
  • Not available for Northern Ireland or UK remote, Highlands & Islands
  • Some larger items may not qualify due to courier restrictions

Remote, Highlands & Islands

  • Courier delivery: £17.50
  • Next-day delivery is not available

Republic of Ireland

  • Free delivery on orders over €250
  • €30 for orders below €250
  • Next-day delivery is not available

International

We currently deliver within the UK and Republic of Ireland only.

Product Restrictions

Certain candles and fragrance items cannot be shipped to Northern Ireland or the Republic of Ireland. Any restrictions will be clearly noted on the relevant product pages.
