Furniture Delivery

Larger furniture pieces are delivered with care through our specialist home delivery partners. Every item is handled with attention, ensuring it arrives safely and ready for your space.

Home Delivery Service

  • Delivered by a two‑person team
  • Brought into your home and placed in the room of your choice
  • All packaging removed and responsibly recycled
  • Delivery dates arranged directly with you for convenience

Delivery Charges

  • Calculated at checkout based on your postcode and item size
  • Visible before you complete your order
  • One charge applied per order, regardless of quantity

If your order includes both furniture and smaller courier‑sent items, your delivery charge will reflect the furniture delivery service only. Items may arrive separately depending on scheduling.
